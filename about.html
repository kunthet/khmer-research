បេសកកម្មរបស់យើង — Our Mission

KhmerResearch.com is the first AI-powered digital library dedicated to translating and summarizing global academic research into Khmer. We believe that knowledge should not be locked behind language barriers. Our mission is to make world-class research accessible to every Cambodian student, educator, researcher, and curious mind.

How It Works

Our automated pipeline processes academic papers through several stages:

  • Source: We discover open-access papers from databases like OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, CrossRef, and DOAJ.
  • Analyze: AI reads the full paper and classifies it by document type (research paper, report, or textbook) and academic category.
  • Translate & Summarize: The AI generates a comprehensive bilingual summary including executive summary, methodology, key findings, a technical glossary, and further reading suggestions.
  • Quality Check: An automated completeness check ensures all required sections are present. Incomplete articles are automatically re-processed.
  • Publish: The final article is rendered as a bilingual HTML page and indexed for search.

AI Transparency

We believe in being transparent about our use of AI. All summaries and translations on KhmerResearch.com are generated by artificial intelligence (currently Google Gemini). While we strive for accuracy, AI-generated content may contain errors or simplifications.

We strongly encourage readers to cross-reference with the original source papers, which are linked on every article page. Our content should be treated as a "first draft understanding" — a starting point for deeper research, not a replacement for reading the original work.
